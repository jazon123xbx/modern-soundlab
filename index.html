Explain It

Question 1: How does the app change a sound wave into binary?

First, it takes samples of the wave. Next, it rounds each sample to a level. Lastly, it changes each level into binary.

Question 2: For Test A, which gives more detail: 2 bits or 6 bits?

6 bits gives more detail because it has 64 possible levels. With 2 bits, there are only 4 levels.

Question 3: Which test has the longest wavelength?

Test B has the longest wavelength because its frequency is the lowest (200 Hz). When frequency goes up, wavelength goes down.

Question 4: What was the hardest stage to build, and how did you fix it?

The hardest part was converting the wave samples into the correct binary numbers because even a small mistake could change the result. I fixed it by checking the formula step by step and comparing my results with the given examples.
